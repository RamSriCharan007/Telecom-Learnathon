TotalCharges Whitespace Resolution Audit (Rule R2)

Handling 11 records in raw Telco CSV containing whitespace string " ".

Deterministic Imputation

Inspection Finding: All 11 records with whitespace TotalCharges had tenure = 0 (brand new customers signed up within current billing cycle who have not yet received their first monthly invoice).

Formula: TotalCharges = tenure * MonthlyCharges = 0 * MonthlyCharges = $0.00

This deterministic domain imputation strictly matches physical reality without calculating global means/medians, preventing cross-split leakage.

Encapsulated ColumnTransformer Architecture

Every feature engineering step executes inside the training loop to guarantee zero validation data snooping.

# Numerical Pipeline (tenure, MonthlyCharges, TotalCharges)
num_pipeline = Pipeline([('imputer', SimpleImputer(strategy='median')), ('scaler', StandardScaler())])

# Categorical Pipeline (16 demographic & service features)
cat_pipeline = Pipeline([('imputer', SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent')), ('ohe', OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore', sparse_output=False))])

# Combined Preprocessor
preprocessor = ColumnTransformer(transformers=[('num', num_pipeline, num_cols), ('cat', cat_pipeline, cat_cols)])

The High-Tenure, High-Charge False Positive Paradox

Investigating loyal subscribers who trigger false alarms due to premium monthly invoices.

$11,300 Wasted Budget
Cohort Size 226 Users Tenure ≥ 36m & Charges ≥ $70
Average Tenure 49.5 Months 4.1+ Years of Loyalty
Average Monthly Bill $98.52 / mo 99.6% Fiber Optic • 73% M2M
Multiple Lines 85.8% High Ecosystem Inertia
The Mechanism: The model observes high monthly bills ($98.52/mo), Fiber Optic service (99.6%), and Month-to-Month contracts (73.0%), causing it to output high churn risk (p = 0.644). However, these customers have 4+ years of loyalty and multiple active lines, creating strong ecosystem inertia.
